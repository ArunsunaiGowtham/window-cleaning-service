Service Standards & Commitments

Terms of Service

Clear and transparent service terms governing our streak-free pure-water window care, rain guarantees, and scheduling.

Last Updated: October 2026
Standard: 000 PPM Pure Water legal@crystalclean.com

1 Scope of Window Cleaning Services

CrystalClean delivers professional exterior and interior window cleaning, architectural glass detailing, and pure-water rinses:

  • Pure Water Technology: We clean using reverse-osmosis, deionized water filtered to 000 PPM, allowing glass to dry completely spot-free without chemical residues.
  • Frame & Sill Detailing: All standard services include thorough brushing and rinsing of window frames, sills, and weep holes.
  • Specialized Add-ons: Skylights, solariums, solar panel de-soiling, and screen deep-washes are performed as defined in your service estimate.

2 48-Hour Streak-Free Rain Guarantee

We stand behind our pure-water cleaning results with a straightforward rain assurance policy:

  • Complimentary Touch-Up: If weather rain events cause visible spotting within 48 hours of service completion, contact us for a free exterior re-rinse.
  • Notice Window: Weather claims must be submitted within 48 hours via your customer dashboard priority alert or phone hotline.

3 Property Access & Client Preparation

To ensure timely completion and safety, clients agree to prepare the service location as follows:

  • Perimeter Access: Ensure side gates are unlocked or share gate digital codes prior to the technician's scheduled arrival window.
  • Pet & Obstacle Safety: Keep household pets safely secured indoors and clear window perimeter walkways of delicate garden decor.
  • Interior Sills: For interior cleaning appointments, please raise window blinds and remove fragile items from window sills.

4 Transparent Pricing & Payment Terms

We operate on 100% upfront pricing without hidden technician travel surcharges:

  • Fixed Estimate Guarantee: The quote approved prior to technician dispatch is the final price. Heavy post-construction cement or paint scraping is quoted separately before work begins.
  • Payment Timing: Invoices are settled upon job completion via auto-pay or online customer portal card payment.

5 Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

We understand schedules change. To respect dispatch routes, we kindly ask for reasonable notice:

  • 24-Hour Notice: Rescheduling or cancelling with at least 24 hours' notice incurs zero charge and can be completed directly in your portal.
  • Locked-Out / Late Notice: Same-day cancellations after a mobile van has dispatched may be subject to a nominal $35 travel fee.

6 Insurance, Safety & Pre-Existing Conditions

Your property is completely protected under our rigorous commercial coverage:

  • $5,000,000 Liability Insurance: CrystalClean carries comprehensive liability coverage and workers' compensation for all field crew.
  • Pre-Existing Glass Conditions: Pre-existing glass scratches, broken thermal double-glazing seals (internal fogging), or hard-water etching will be photographed and reported prior to washing.

Have Questions About Your Service Agreement?

Our customer support team is available Monday through Saturday to answer any questions.
