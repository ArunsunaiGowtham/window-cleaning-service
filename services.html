Engineered Precision

Our 4-Stage Pure-Water Protocol

Every pane receives a systematic multi-step clean engineered to dissolve mineral grime and evaporate 100% spotless without chemical films.

01

Pre-Inspection & Scrub

Technicians inspect seal integrity and loosen cobwebs, moss, bird droppings, and accumulated debris from frame extrusions and weep holes.

02

0-TDS Pure Water Wash

Dual-trim boar’s hair & soft nylon brushes agitate microscopic pollutants using 000 PPM deionised water, breaking ionic surface bonds.

03

Hydrophobic Sheeting

Purified water cascades across the glass surface, encapsulating loosened particulates and flowing down smoothly without droplet rings.

04

Sills & 48h Warranty

Exterior sills and ledges are hand-dried with lint-free microfibre, final quality inspection signed off, and backed by our free 48-Hour Rain Guarantee.

Standard tap water contains 150–350 PPM of dissolved minerals like calcium and lime that leave white crust spots when droplets evaporate. Our 4-stage van filtration system purifies municipal water down to 000 PPM. Devoid of minerals, pure water acts as a natural solvent, lifting dirt and evaporating into thin air without leaving any residue or spots behind.

For exterior cleans, you do not need to be present as long as side gates are unlocked, exterior water spigots are accessible, and pets are kept inside. For interior window cleaning, we simply ask that window sills are cleared of ornaments or blinds are pulled up. Our technicians wear protective shoe booties and place clean drop sheets on all interior flooring.

Because pure water cleans away all detergent residues, clean rainwater simply sheets off your glass without sticking. However, if strong winds or an unexpected storm blows dirt or mud onto your windows within 48 hours of service, we will return and re-rinse your exterior glass completely free of charge.
