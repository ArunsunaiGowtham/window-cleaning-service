The Science of Pure Water: Why Zero TDS Prevents Spots

  • Marcus Vance (Master Window Tech)
  • 28 September 2026
  • 7 min read
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. The Science of Pure Water

Have you ever wondered why washing windows with a garden hose leaves behind milky, stubborn spots? The culprit is not the glass itself—it is the invisible mineral load hiding inside municipal tap water.

1. Understanding TDS: Total Dissolved Solids

Every municipal tap water supply carries dissolved minerals such as calcium carbonate, magnesium, sodium, potassium, and silica. In urban areas, tap water typically tests between 180 and 420 PPM (Parts Per Million) TDS. When this water dries on exterior glass, the pure H₂O molecules evaporate into the air, leaving 100% of the solid mineral crystals bonded directly to the silica surface.

2. The 4-Stage Deionisation Process

At CrystalClean, each of our mobile service vans is outfitted with a custom industrial water purification skid. We run raw water through four rigorous mechanical and chemical filtration stages before it touches your windows:

Stage 1: 5-Micron Sediment Filter

Removes suspended particulate matter including rust, silt, pipe scale, and sand.

Stage 2: Carbon Block Pre-Filter

Neutralizes chlorine and chloramines that would otherwise degrade fragile reverse osmosis membranes.

Stage 3: RO Membrane Separation

Forces water at 80 PSI through a semi-permeable spiral membrane, stripping 95%–98% of all dissolved minerals.

Stage 4: Nuclear-Grade DI Resin

Anion and cation mixed-bed exchange resin polishes water down to an exact 000 PPM (zero parts per million).

3. Why 0-TDS Water Acts as a Natural Dirt Magnet

Water in its pure, deionised state is chemically "hungry." Because all ionic charges and dissolved mineral salts have been stripped away, pure water behaves as an aggressive natural solvent. When agitated across glass using our soft boar's hair and nylon brushes, the pure water molecules naturally attach to atmospheric dust, bird droppings, pollen, and spider residue, suspending the grime effortlessly without requiring detergents, surfactants, or chemical soaps.

No Soap Residue = Slower Re-Soiling

Traditional bucket-and-squeegee methods rely on detergent dish soap. Even when wiped, a microscopic invisible surfactant film remains on the glass. This sticky film acts like electrostatic tape for road dust and pollen. With 0-PPM pure water, there is ZERO residue left behind, meaning your windows stay clean up to twice as long!

4. The Final Spotless Air Dry

After our telescopic carbon-fibre poles thoroughly brush and rinse the glass from top to bottom, the remaining pure water creates a uniform sheeting action across the glass pane. As it dries naturally under sunlight or gentle breeze, there are literally zero solids left to deposit. The glass dries with 100% optical clarity—crystal clean from corner to corner.
