Water Purification & Technology

Municipal tap water contains 150–400 PPM of dissolved minerals like calcium, lime, and magnesium. When tap water dries, the water evaporates leaving hard mineral spots. Our van-mounted multi-stage filtration system uses sediment filtration, carbon pre-filters, reverse osmosis, and deionising resin to strip 100% of dissolved solids to 000 PPM. Pure water acts as a natural solvent, lifting dirt and evaporating completely streak-free.

Because pure water contains zero dissolved solids or soapy surfactants, wiping with a rubber squeegee is actually counterproductive. The pure water forms an unbroken sheet that evaporates naturally into the air within minutes, leaving flawless optical clarity.

Because our wash removes all sticky detergent residues, clean rainwater runs right off your glass. However, if strong winds or an unexpected storm blows dirt or mud onto your windows within 48 hours of service, we will return and re-rinse your exterior glass free of charge!
