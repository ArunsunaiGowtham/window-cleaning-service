Client Protection & Data Trust

Privacy Policy

How CrystalClean safeguards your personal information, on-site property privacy, and paperless service records.

Effective Date: October 2026
Applicability: Residential & Commercial privacy@crystalclean.com

1 Information We Collect

To deliver zero-TDS pure-water exterior cleaning and accurate quotes, CrystalClean collects limited, necessary information:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone number, and email address for appointment confirmations and crew arrival windows.
  • Service Property Details: Physical address, pane count, architectural facade notes, and optional gate/access codes provided for rear yard access.
  • Quality Assurance Records: Timestamped pure-water TDS ppm telemetry and exterior photo records taken solely of glass surfaces to document spotless completion.

2 How We Use Your Data

Your details are strictly used to fulfill and guarantee our exterior cleaning commitments:

  • Crew Dispatch & Arrival Tracking: Routing our mobile deionized water vans to your property efficiently and updating arrival notifications.
  • 48-Hour Rain Guarantee Verification: Confirming completed service dates in case local weather requires a complimentary rain re-rinse.
  • Paperless Billing: Delivering itemized receipts, customer portal access, and digital invoice statements.

3 On-Site Property Privacy & Equipment Safety

We adhere to rigorous protocols to protect the privacy of your home and commercial property while on site:

  • Zero Recording Apparatus: Our water-fed carbon poles and squeegees are strictly cleaning apparatus with no optical cameras or surveillance hardware.
  • Interior Window Privacy: For interior glass care, all technicians are background-checked and trained to respect curtains, blinds, and household boundaries.
  • Access Code Protection: Gate combinations or entry codes are stored encrypted and revealed only to the lead technician on the day of dispatch.

4 Data Security & Payment Processing

We prioritize the security of all client transactions and digital account data:

  • No Third-Party Selling: We never sell, lease, or monetize customer contact lists or addresses to marketing aggregators.
  • PCI-DSS Compliant Payments: Online card transactions and auto-pay records are handled directly through certified payment gateways. We never store raw card numbers.
