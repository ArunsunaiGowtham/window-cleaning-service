The CrystalClean Difference

Why Our Pure Water Cleans Better Than Soap

Ordinary window washing uses soapy water that leaves a microscopic sticky film—attracting dirt within days. Our multi-stage deionised water system dries with zero chemical spots.

  • 0 PPM Deionised Pure Water

    Reverse-osmosis filtered water attracts minerals like a magnet and evaporates 100% streak-free.

  • Ground-Operated Reach up to 75ft

    Ultra-lightweight telescopic carbon fiber poles protect your lawn, roof tiles, and window sills from ladder damage.

  • Fully Insured & Background Checked

    $5M public liability coverage, OSHA/HSE compliant, and verified uniformed technicians on every visit.

  • 48-Hour Rain & Streak Guarantee

    If rain spots your windows or you notice any imperfection within 48 hours, we return for a free re-rinse.
