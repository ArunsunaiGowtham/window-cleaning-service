About CrystalClean Window Services

Pioneers in zero-TDS pure water glass technology, delivering spotless, streak-free views to thousands of residential and commercial clients.

Our Story

Built on a Obsession for 100% Streak-Free Clarity

In 2012, our founder recognized that traditional window washers were using soapy bucket water that left a sticky invisible film, causing windows to attract dust within days while dangerous ladders chipped paint and dented gutters.

CrystalClean introduced high-reach carbon fiber water-fed poles powered by multi-stage reverse osmosis and deionisation filtration. Today, our directly employed, certified technicians clean thousands of residential homes and commercial skyscrapers with zero ladder hazards and unmatched optical clarity.

Mission

To flood every home and office with maximum natural light through non-toxic, spotless pure water cleaning and friendly, dependable service.

Vision

To be the most trusted, safety-compliant glass care and facade maintenance provider across residential communities and commercial skylines.

Fleet Innovation & Hardware

Inside Our Mobile Pure-Water Laboratories

We don't rely on tap water or soapy buckets. Each vehicle in our fleet is a self-contained, high-pressure water purification facility engineered to deliver mineral-free water to every pane.

Dual Industrial RO Membranes

Strips 98% of hard water minerals, iron, and calcium under 150 PSI pressure before deionization polishing, preventing scale bonding.

Nuclear-Grade Virgin DI Resin

Removes all residual trace ions down to absolute 000 PPM Total Dissolved Solids, transforming water into an aggressive natural solvent.

75-Foot Carbon-Fiber Gardiner Poles

Enables ultra-precise, ground-based facade and skylight access up to 6 storeys with zero ladder dents, chipping, or perimeter hazards.

Digital Micro-Flow Regulation

Micro-controlled onboard Shurflo pumps meter pure water flow per minute, avoiding water waste and protecting delicate pane seals.

The 4 Pillars of CrystalClean Peace of Mind

We founded CrystalClean to raise the bar for professional window care. Here are the four ironclad principles that guide every single appointment.

100% W2 Direct Staff

We never subcontract to third parties. Every technician on your property is a vetted, background-checked, and directly employed CrystalClean professional.

48-Hour Rain Guarantee

If unexpected rain, wind storms, or mineral spotting occurs within 48 hours of your clean, notify dispatch and we'll re-clean affected exterior panes free of charge.

Zero Ladder Damage

Operating lightweight carbon-fiber poles from ground level means zero ladders leaning on delicate aluminum gutters, fascia boards, or flower gardens.

Eco-Pure H2O Chemistry

Zero detergents, soaps, phosphates, or toxic solvents. Safe for children, pets, koi ponds, and landscaping, leaving zero sticky residue to attract dirt.

Frequently Asked Questions About CrystalClean

Have questions regarding our safety protocols, equipment, or service guarantees? Find answers directly from our operations team below.

Subcontractors typically bring inconsistent equipment, varied chemical mixtures, and questionable insurance policies. At CrystalClean, every technician is a W2 employee who has completed 120 hours of mentored training, passed rigorous background checks, and operates our proprietary zero-TDS van purification rigs under $10M comprehensive liability coverage.
Household dish detergents and chemical window sprays leave behind an invisible surfactant film. This sticky microscopic layer creates an electrostatic charge that binds pollen and dust within days. Deionized water has had 100% of mineral ions removed (000 PPM TDS). In this state, water acts as an aggressive natural solvent that dissolves dirt, sheets off smoothly, and evaporates completely streak-free.
CrystalClean maintains $10M public liability coverage, full commercial property damage insurance, and comprehensive worker's compensation.
