Spring Conservatory & Skylight Glass Care Guide

  • Liam Chen (Specialist Glass Restoration Tech)
  • 30 August 2026
  • 8 min read
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Conservatory & Skylight Care

Conservatories, sunrooms, and modern architectural roof lanterns are engineered to flood your living space with uplifting natural light. But due to their shallow pitch, overhead glass panels accumulate organic matter faster than vertical windows.

1. The Biological Build-Up on Overhead Glass

Because roof glass rests at angles between 10° and 35°, rainwater drains slowly. Standing droplets pool around exterior rubber gaskets and aluminium glazing bars, creating the ideal damp micro-habitat for green moss, lichen, black algae spores, and fallen tree sap. Left unaddressed over a winter season, lichen filaments secrete oxalic acids that can irreversibly pit glass surfaces and degrade watertight perimeter seals.

2. Why You Should Never Walk on Glass Roofs

Homeowners often attempt DIY cleaning by leaning ladders against delicate plastic guttering or stepping directly onto timber roof spars. Even toughened laminate glass panels are not designed to support dynamic human foot loads, and wet glass has a near-zero friction coefficient. Professional conservatory washing must be carried out safely from ground level or secured crawl-boards using extended carbon-composite water-fed poles.

Protecting Self-Cleaning & Low-E Solar Coatings

Many contemporary skylights feature factory-applied titanium dioxide (TiO₂) photocatalytic "self-cleaning" coatings. Abrasive scouring pads, alkaline detergents, or metal razor scrapers will permanently strip these ultra-thin microscopic layers. CrystalClean uses zero-detergent pure water and soft flagged monofilament brushes that protect delicate coatings 100%.

3. The 3-Step Conservatory Restoration Process

Step 1: Gutter & Valley Flush

We clear decomposed leaf litter and moss dams from box gutters and finial roof ridges.

Step 2: Gentle Biocide Soak

Organic lichens are broken down using pH-neutral, plant-safe organic surfactants.

Step 3: 0-TDS Pure Rinse

Telescopic poles deliver heated deionised water, leaving gleaming glass that dries spot-free.

4. Frequency Recommendations

For optimal thermal efficiency and maximum natural illumination, we recommend a thorough conservatory roof clean twice annually—once in early spring to clear winter algae, and once in autumn after leaf fall. This keeps rubber seals pliant and prevents costly water leak repairs.
